Corporate Overview & Heritage

About KSR International Trade Manufacturing

A premier Indian spice manufacturing, processing, and export enterprise dedicated to delivering authentic quality, consistency, and transparent value to the global food sector.

Bridging Indian Agricultural Abundance with Global Food Standards

Founded and strategically situated at Ambad, Jalna (Maharashtra, India), KSR International Trade Manufacturing operates with an unwavering commitment to the international spice trade. We specialize in the sourcing, hygienic sorting, grading, cool grinding, and containerized export of whole spices, pure spice powders, and bespoke spice blends.

The enterprise is steered by Founder and Managing Director Mr. Kunal Rathod, who brings over 7 years of hands-on export-import industry experience. Under his stewardship, KSR was established not merely as a merchant intermediary, but as a dedicated, manufacturing-centric supplier equipped to satisfy the technical requirements of industrial food processors, millers, and retail distributors across the Middle East, Southeast Asia, Africa, and Europe.

Our Core Positioning

KSR is an Indian spice manufacturing, processing and export company focused on supplying quality Indian spices to international buyers, importers, distributors, food processors and seasoning manufacturers.

Maharashtra and its contiguous states represent some of the world's most acclaimed agricultural belts for turmeric, red chilli, cumin, and coriander. By establishing manufacturing close to primary procurement centers and linking directly with primary ocean container terminals at JNPT (Nhava Sheva, Mumbai), KSR ensures optimum freshness, competitive pricing, and streamlined supply chains.

Factory & Operations

Manufacturing & Processing Workflow

Our Ambad, Jalna facility operates an integrated manufacturing process designed to eliminate foreign contaminants, preserve natural colour and volatile oils, and achieve exact buyer particle specifications.

01

Raw Material Sourcing

Direct farm and mandi sourcing across premier growing belts in Maharashtra & India.

02

Raw Material Inspection

Primary sampling checking moisture content, aroma, visual purity, and foreign organic matter.

03

Cleaning and Sorting

Multi-deck vibro-screen cleaners, air aspirators, and magnetic separators remove dust, stones, and chaff.

04

Grading and Processing

Precision size grading (cardamom millimeter screens, whole chilli length, stem sorting, bold seed separation).

05

Grinding & Powder Milling

Temperature-monitored pulverizing mills preserving delicate volatile oils and target mesh consistency.

06

Quality Inspection

Rigorous batch quality audits: moisture checks, sieve analysis (mesh size), colour consistency, and aroma.

07

Packaging & Sealing

Hygienic packing into moisture-barrier PP sacks, Kraft multi-wall bags, or private label pouches.

08

Final Dispatch & Port Transit

Container stuffing, palletization, fumigation, customs clearance, and road transit to JNPT Nhava Sheva (Mumbai).

Complete Export Documentation Checklist

Certificate of Origin (COO)

Issued by authorized chambers of commerce confirming authentic Indian agricultural origin for preferential tariff treatment.

Commercial Invoice & Packing List

Itemized documentation specifying gross/net weight, lot numbers, container numbers, and seal counts.

Bill of Lading (B/L)

Clean on Board ocean B/L issued by global shipping lines (Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, Hapag-Lloyd).

Phytosanitary Certificate

Official plant quarantine certification verifying cargo is pest-free and compliant with importing destination quarantine laws.

Certificate of Analysis (COA)

Batch testing reports confirming physical and chemical parameters (moisture, volatile oils, curcumin, SHU).

Fumigation Certificate

Professional methyl bromide (MBr) or phosphine (ALP) fumigation certificate verifying treatment of cargo and container.
