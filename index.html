About KSR International

Pioneering Agricultural Manufacturing & Global Supply Chain

Headquartered with integrated processing units in Ambad (Maharashtra), KSR International Trade Manufacturing is dedicated to delivering high-grade spices, grain commodities, and agro produce directly from harvest farmers to commercial importers, wholesalers, and food processing industries across the globe.

With deep local sourcing dominance across Maharashtra, Gujarat, and South Indian spice belts, we ensure optimal natural aroma, high active ingredient values (Curcumin, Capsaicin, Volatile Oil), and strict zero-adulteration protocols compliant with US-FDA, European, and Gulf standards.

Farm-Gate Sourcing

Direct contracts with vetted agricultural cooperatives eliminating middlemen.

JNPT Port Logistics

Quick multimodal transit to Nhava Sheva (JNPT) Mumbai port for swift vessel loading.

Quality Integrity

Custom Packaging

Manufacturing & Infrastructure

State-of-the-Art Processing Unit

Automated Grading & Sorting

High-precision optical sortex and gravity separator machinery sorting out immature berries, foreign matter, and dust particles.

  • 99.5% Purity Sortex Grade
  • Magnetic Metal Detection

Cold Grinding & Milling

Cryogenic and low-temperature pulverization prevents heat degradation, retaining essential natural spice oils, aroma, and vibrant colors.

  • High Volatile Oil Retention
  • Uniform Mesh Size (60 to 120 Mesh)

Hygienic Warehousing & Packing

Moisture-controlled clean storage bays with automated nitrogen-flushed and vacuum bagging units protecting export cargo during sea journeys.

  • 25kg / 50kg Multiwall Paper & PP Bags
  • Fumigation and Desiccant Sealed
