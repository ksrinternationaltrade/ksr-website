About Our Company

A Direct Spice Manufacturing & Export House

KSR INTERNATIONAL TRADE MANUFACTURING is an Indian spice manufacturing, processing, and export company operating from Ambad, Jalna, Maharashtra. We specialize in supplying premium-grade whole spices, finely milled powders, and customized seasoning blends to international importers, distributors, food processors, and seasoning manufacturers worldwide.

Backed by 7+ years of international export-import industry experience, KSR is positioned firmly as an authentic manufacturing and processing enterprise rather than a generic trading intermediary. We oversee every critical step—from farm-gate sourcing in verified agricultural belts of Maharashtra and Andhra Pradesh to rigorous cleaning, grading, grinding, hygienic packaging, and container stuffing.

Our operations prioritize strict adherence to contract specifications, moisture and foreign matter control, competitive export pricing, and transparent, professional buyer relationships built for long-term growth.

Infrastructure & Workflow

Manufacturing & Processing Facility

Located at Ambad, Jalna, Maharashtra—the agro-commodity heartland—our facility processes and packages export-quality spices under strict hygienic and process controls.

01

Raw Material Sourcing

Direct farm-gate aggregation from certified growing belts in Maharashtra, Telangana, and Gujarat.

02

Raw Material Inspection

Incoming lot testing for moisture levels, foreign matter, color retention, and physical integrity.

03

Cleaning & Sortex Sorting

Air-gravity de-stoning, magnetic separators, and optical Sortex color-sorting up to 99% purity.

04

Grading & Sizing

Calibrated mechanical sieving to deliver uniform mesh, finger length, or seed bold count.

05

Grinding & Pulverizing

Low-temperature hammer milling and pulverizing lines that protect natural volatile oils and aroma.

06

Quality Analysis (COA)

Comprehensive batch testing verifying microbiological standards, ash content, and curcumin/SHU values.

07

Export Packaging

25/50 kg PP bags, multi-layer Kraft paper, jute with inner poly liners, and client private label pouches.

08

Container Stuffing & Dispatch

Dry, desiccated 20ft & 40ft container stuffing with palletization and seamless transit to JNPT Nhava Sheva.
